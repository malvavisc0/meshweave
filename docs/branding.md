## Logo Directions — Meshweave

Concept directions
1) Woven lattice monogram — Interlaced “MW” built on a 3×3 or 4×4 grid; use negative space to imply weave; must scale down to 16px as a favicon.
2) Node–link weave — Diamond/hex mesh with 2–3 accent nodes; subtle depth via 2-tone; works as a square avatar and app icon.
3) Loom stroke motif — Two interleaving bands crossing at 45°/60°; friendly motion path for micro-animations on submit/progress.

Wordmark
- Typeface: geometric/neo-grotesk sans with slight warmth (General Sans, Inter, Satoshi, Plus Jakarta Sans). Alternate: customize a rounded “W” to echo the weave.
- Letter tweaks: tighten “shw” and “wea” joins; open counters; keep terminals straight (avoid excessive humanist contrast).
- Weight/spacing: Regular/Semibold for UI; optical kerning; align cap height to icon for balanced lockup.

Color
- Primary: Ink Navy #0F172A or Graphite #111827
- Accent: Teal Weave #14B8A6 or Electric Cyan #06B6D4
- Neutral: Fog #E5E7EB, Paper #F8FAFC
- Dark mode: allow monochrome icon; ensure 4.5:1 contrast minimum for text.

Grid and sizing
- Icon built on 8px grid; 2px strokes at 24px; round caps/joins; minimum clear space = icon stroke ×1.
- Favicon: reduce intersections to 2–3; avoid micro-gaps.
- Social avatar: square 1:1; thicken strokes for 32–64px sizes.

Do / Don’t
- Do: maintain intersection symmetry; test on light/dark; keep to 1–2 brand colors.
- Don’t: hairline strokes under 1.5px at 24px; busy mesh noise; gradient clutter.

Deliverables checklist
- SVG icon and wordmark; horizontal/stacked lockups; mono/duo color variants.
- Favicon (16/32/48), social avatar (512), app icon (1024), and open-graph banner.
- Usage guidelines: clear-space, min-size, color specs, and misuses.

## Domain and Trademark Checklist

- Domain
  - Check .com first; if taken, evaluate .io fallback
  - Screen for squatting or inactive parked pages
  - Ensure clear pronunciation and spellability

- Trademark / Conflicts
  - Quick search in major jurisdictions for identical/close names in adjacent categories
  - Avoid overlaps with large, adjacent tech brands
  - Prefer distinct visual/phonetic signature

- Linguistics
  - Sanity‑check across EN/ES/DE/FR/IT for unintended meanings
  - Avoid harsh consonant clusters that hinder pronunciation
