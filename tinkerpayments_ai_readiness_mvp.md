## 🚀 Quick Wins (Fix These First)

1. **Add author information to articles** (+5-10 points)
   - *Why*: AI systems use author signals to assess content credibility. Without author schema, your articles look anonymous.
   - *How*: Add author JSON-LD to article pages with `name`, `url`, and `sameAs` properties.
2. **Increase average content depth** (+5-10 points)
   - *Why*: AI systems need substantive content to build authority signals. Thin pages get skipped.
   - *How*: Target 500+ words on key pages.
3. **Fix canonical URL mismatches** (+3-5 points)
   - *Why*: A canonical URL tells AI systems which version of your content to trust.
   - *How*: Update canonical tags on pages with mismatches.
