What these reports measure

Every MeshWeave analysis scores a website across three risk lenses. Each lens answers a different question about how AI systems treat your site, and each is scored 0-100 from automated checks across your crawled pages.

The three MeshWeave risk lenses
Lens Question it answers What it checks
AEO — Answer Engine Optimization Can AI extract clean answers from your pages? Schema coverage, heading hierarchy, content structure, freshness
GEO — Generative Engine Optimization Does AI see enough trust signals to recommend you? Entity consistency, E-E-A-T signals, crawl access, content depth
AAX — AI Agent Experience Can an AI agent complete tasks on your site? Homepage comprehension, contactability, llms.txt, metadata

Each report also lists prioritized recommendations — the specific fixes that move each lens score fastest. See the full methodology for factor weights and rating bands.

How to read a report

Every analysis follows the same shape, whether it was run five minutes ago or five months ago.

  • Lens scores — AEO, GEO, and AAX composites with rating bands from Poor/Invisible/Opaque to Excellent/Dominant/Fluent.
  • Factor breakdown — each lens is a weighted average of measurable factors (schema coverage, crawl access, E-E-A-T signals, …), so you can see exactly which signal is holding a score back.
  • Interpretation — a plain-language diagnosis of the score profile and which lens to fix first.
  • Recommendations — prioritized fixes with estimated impact, sorted by what moves the composite fastest.

Reports also flag what a free scan cannot measure — citation frequency, query-match relevance, and capture rate require real-world data, and the report says so rather than guessing.
