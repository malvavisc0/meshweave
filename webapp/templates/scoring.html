What makes a website AI-friendly?

This page explains how MeshWeave computes the Reachable, Answerable, and Actionable checks, how missing factors are handled, and how to interpret each score as a diagnostic signal rather than a guaranteed outcome.

How to read this page

MeshWeave scores three checks in the agent's journey order: Reachable, Answerable, and Actionable. They are diagnostic views of how AI-friendly your site is. They show where AI systems may fail to reach, answer from, or act on your content — the website itself, not outside outcomes.

Shared composite logic

All three checks use a weighted composite with re-normalization. Factors with no score are excluded, the remaining weights are redistributed, and the result is calibrated to compress inflated upper-range scores.

  • Weighted composite — Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weight) using only factors with non-None scores
  • Re-normalization — when a factor is unmeasurable, its weight is redistributed across the remaining factors
  • Calibration — the composite is passed through a power curve that compresses the upper range, so near-perfect scores require near-perfect factors
Rule Behavior
Weighted composite Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weight) using only factors with non-None scores
Missing factors Weights are re-normalized across available factors
Calibration curve 100 × (composite / 100)^1.15 compresses the upper range
Output Capped at 100, rounded to 1 decimal place

Reachable — machine context

Reachable measures site-wide machine context: whether AI agents can reach the relevant material and reconcile the business identity, evidence, and claims across the site. Crawl access, entity consistency, content depth, and site-wide evidence carry the score — presence of third-party markers does not.

Answerable — answer extractability

Answerable measures how well your content is structured so AI agents can extract direct answers from it. A grounded answerability test carries material weight in this check: it runs a fixed decision-critical benchmark over your crawled pages and scores only answers its pages support.

Actionable — agent actionability

Actionable measures whether a structured AI evaluation can understand your site, assess the offer, and identify a credible next step from key pages. It does not test transactions or operate an interactive browser agent.
