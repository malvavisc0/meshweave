Three ways AI is steering buyers to your competitors

Every time a buyer asks AI for a vendor, one of three things decides who wins. First, AI can or cannot quote your answer. Second, AI sees you as credible or overlooks you. Third, agents can or cannot act on your site. These three lenses — AEO, GEO, and AAX — map to the exact risks costing you deals right now.

Risk Lens What Failure Looks Like Business Consequence
AEO
Answer Engine Optimization		 AI cannot extract clear, trustworthy answers from your content. Your brand is not quoted when buyers ask questions. Competitors own the answer.
GEO
Generative Engine Optimization		 AI systems see you as less authoritative and credible than competitors. Your brand is overlooked or replaced in recommendations. You lose customers without seeing it.
AAX
AI Agent Experience		 AI systems cannot confidently identify the offer, understand the content, or find a credible next step. AI assistants are less likely to recommend your site when buyers ask for options.

Together, these lenses show where your site is vulnerable to AI invisibility — and which fixes protect revenue first.

Frequently asked questions

What does MeshWeave do?

MeshWeave finds and fixes the website weaknesses that make your brand invisible, untrusted, or unusable to AI systems. We show where answer engines, LLMs, and AI agents fail to quote, recommend, or act on your site, allowing your competitors to win.

Is this an SEO tool?

No. Traditional SEO focuses on how humans find you through search engines. MeshWeave focuses on whether AI systems can understand, trust, cite, recommend, and use your website. The risk is not ranking lower — it is being skipped entirely when buyers ask AI, while your competitors steal your visibility.
