Frequently asked questions

What does MeshWeave do?

MeshWeave finds and fixes the website weaknesses that make your brand invisible, untrusted, or unusable to AI systems. We show where answer engines, LLMs, and AI agents fail to quote, recommend, or act on your site.

Is this an SEO tool?

No. Traditional SEO focuses on how humans find you through search engines. MeshWeave focuses on whether AI systems can understand, trust, cite, recommend, and use your website. The risk is not ranking lower — it is being skipped entirely in AI-mediated discovery.

Who is MeshWeave for?

SEO and content teams, product and web teams, marketing and growth teams, founders, operators, agencies, and consultants responsible for making sure a brand remains discoverable and usable as buyers shift toward AI-assisted research and decision-making.

What will I learn from running an analysis?

  • Whether AI systems understand what you do — or are getting it wrong
  • Whether they can extract and cite your best answers
  • Whether AI sees you as a credible option — or you are invisible in recommendations
  • Whether AI agents can navigate your site and complete actions — or abandon mid-task
  • What to fix to stop losing ground

Can I share analysis results with my team?

Yes. Signed-in users get private reports with shareable URLs, so results are easy to review with clients or teammates.
