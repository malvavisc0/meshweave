Frequently asked questions

What are AEO, GEO, and AAX scores?

Metric What It Measures
AEO How well your site captures Google AI Overviews and featured snippets
GEO Visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity
AAX How well AI agents can navigate and act on your site

Each report includes detailed factors, issue explanations, and recommended next steps.

Why does AI search matter?

Traditional search shows a list of links. AI engines give direct answers — and they choose which sources to cite.

If you're not optimized for all three, you're missing the fastest-growing channel for discovery.

What data do you need?

Just a URL. The engine auto-scores:

  • Schema coverage
  • Content structure
  • Entity signals
  • LLM crawl accessibility
  • Content depth

Optionally add Search Console data or voice results for a complete score.

Are results public or private?

  • Signed-in users: Private by default
  • Anonymous runs: Public (can be claimed after signing in)

Can results be shared?

Yes. Each analysis has a unique URL. Shared pages show score gauges and factor breakdowns — no account needed.

Is the analysis accurate?

Our engine crawls and analyzes your site the same way major AI models do, checking schema, content structure, entity signals, and crawl accessibility.

What does better AI visibility lead to?

  • Be understood — AI engines can interpret your content and brand correctly
  • Be cited — Your pages are more usable as sources in generated answers
  • Be discoverable — You are more likely to appear in AI-driven search surfaces
  • Convert — Users can move from AI answer to site visit to action
