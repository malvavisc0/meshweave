How it works

MeshWeave crawls your site, extracts structured data, and produces two composite scores from measured factors.

Crawl

Enter a domain or URL. MeshWeave crawls the site, collecting raw HTML, JSON-LD structured data, robots.txt policies, llms.txt files, heading hierarchies, content metrics, and entity signals across every page. Pages are fetched in parallel and results are available within seconds.

Analyze

The scoring engine evaluates 12 factors across AEO and GEO — including schema coverage, content structure, entity consistency, LLM crawl accessibility, content depth, and E-E-A-T signals.

Score

Get your AEO score (0–100) and GEO score (0–100) with a full factor breakdown, prioritized recommendations ranked by estimated impact, and a shareable report. Re-analyze after changes to track improvement over time.

Frequently asked questions

What are AEO and GEO scores?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) measures how well your site captures Google AI Overviews and featured snippets. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) measures how visible your content is to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Both scored 0–100.

What data do you need?

Just a URL. The engine auto-scores schema coverage, content structure, entity signals, LLM crawl accessibility, and content depth. Optionally add Search Console data or voice results for a complete score.

Are results public or private?

Signed-in users: private by default. Anonymous runs: public. Public analyses can be claimed after signing in.

Can results be shared?

Yes. Each analysis has a unique URL. Shared pages show score gauges and factor breakdowns — no account needed.
