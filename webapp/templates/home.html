FAQ

What does “Chat with the website” mean?

Ask natural questions about the site. The assistant answers using crawled pages and cites sources.

How do emails and leads get found?

Emails are extracted from published site content and tied to the exact page/section. No guessing; everything is sourced.

What does an analysis include?

A browsable brief in clean Markdown, a lead list with sources, and suggested email openers grounded in the site’s own words.

Are analyses public or private?

Signed-in users keep analyses private. Anonymous runs are public by default.

Can outreach be drafted here?

Yes. Use the integrated assistant to generate a first email based on page context and quotes.

What’s coming next?

Early access: Persona fit and Content Gap insights—AI infers likely buyer persona and site alignment with target ICP.

Can results be shared?

Yes. Each analysis has a unique URL for sharing with team or client.
