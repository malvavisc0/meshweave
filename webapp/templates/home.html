Buyers now ask AI first

94% of B2B buyers used generative AI during their purchase process (6sense, 2025 Buyer Experience Report). The agent answers from what it can read on your website. If your price, audience, or contact path is missing or unclear, it answers without them.

From one URL to a ranked fix list

MeshWeave loads your pages the way an AI agent would and reads the text, metadata, structured data, links, robots.txt rules, and contact details. Each problem it finds is filed under one of the three checks: Reachable, Answerable, or Actionable.

01

We read your site

We render your pages and collect what AI agents can see: content, metadata, structured data, links, crawler rules, and contact information.

02

We find the problems

We sort each problem into Reachable, Answerable, or Actionable, and rank it by how many points fixing it would add to your score.

03

We tell you what to fix

Each problem comes with a specific fix, such as adding missing schema, unblocking an AI crawler, making your company description consistent, or adding a clear contact path.

Three ways AI agents fail on a website

An agent first has to reach your pages, then answer a question from them, then work out what to do next. Each check scores one of those steps from 0 to 100.

Check What failure looks like What you fix
Reachable AI crawlers are blocked, pages are hidden behind scripts, or pages describe the company differently. Crawler access, structured data, and one consistent company description.
Answerable Key facts are missing or vague, so an agent has nothing to quote or has to guess. Plain statements of price, features, audience, and other facts buyers ask about.
Actionable An agent cannot tell what you sell or how to buy, sign up, or contact you. A clear offer and a visible buy or contact path.

Frequently asked questions

What does MeshWeave do?

MeshWeave audits your website for AI agents. It checks whether agents can reach your pages, answer questions from your content, and find what to do next, then gives you a ranked list of fixes.

Is this an SEO tool?

No. Traditional SEO focuses on how people find you through search engines. MeshWeave checks whether AI agents can reach your site, answer from it, and act on it. It complements SEO rather than replacing it. Some call this GEO or AEO. MeshWeave covers the site-side part, not AI visibility tracking.

Who is MeshWeave for?

Agencies and consultants who audit client websites, and in-house web, SEO, and content teams who maintain their own site.

What will I learn from an audit?

You will learn which pages agents cannot reach, which questions your content cannot answer, whether agents can find how to buy or contact you, and which fixes to make first.

Can I share a report with my team or a client?

Yes. Every public report has a shareable link. If you sign in, you can keep reports private instead and compare each run with the previous one.
