What happens when you run an analysis?

01

Analyze your site the way AI systems do

Our crawler inspects access, internal linking, structured data, content structure, entity clarity, page depth, and other signals that determine whether machines can reliably reach and interpret your pages.

The results
  • Be understood — AI systems interpret your website and content correctly
  • Be cited — your pages are easier to use as sources in generated answers
  • Be discoverable — you are more likely to appear in AI-driven search workflows
  • Be usable — agents are less likely to fail when navigating your website

What the analysis checks

AEO, GEO, and AAX help explain whether AI systems can access your content, understand it and use your pages as sources.

Metric Full Name What It Measures
AEO Answer Engine Optimization Whether your content is easy to extract, summarize, and cite in answer-driven search
GEO Generative Engine Optimization How well your site is positioned for discovery in systems like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity
AAX AI Agent Experience Whether AI agents can navigate your site, understand actions, and complete tasks reliably

Together, these scores show whether AI systems can access your content, understand what your company does, cite your pages in generated answers, and successfully navigate your site.

Frequently asked questions

What does our engine do?

It audits how AI systems crawl, understand, cite, and use your site. It shows what machines can read, what they miss, and what to fix first.

Is this an SEO tool?

Partly — but more specifically, it helps you understand whether AI systems can crawl, understand, cite, and use your site, then tells you what to fix first.

What questions can be answered?

  • Can LLMs crawl my site reliably?
  • Can they understand what my company does?
  • Can they extract useful answers from my pages?
  • Can agents follow links, interpret structure, and complete actions?
  • What should my team fix first?

Can I share analysis results with my team?

Yes. Signed-in users get private reports with shareable URLs. Perfect for client presentations or team collaboration.

Is the analysis accurate?

We simulate the signals that shape whether AI systems can crawl, understand, and cite your site. It's a practical model of machine-readability — not a claim about any provider's internal ranking.
