Three ways AI can fail to use your website

AEO measures answer extraction, GEO measures generative discovery signals, and AAX measures whether a structured AI evaluation can understand your offer and identify a credible next step. Together, they show what the crawl can verify and what still needs human observation.

Risk Lens What Failure Looks Like Business Consequence
AEO
Answer Engine Optimization		 AI cannot extract clear, trustworthy answers from your content. Your pages may be harder for answer engines to extract and reuse.
GEO
Generative Engine Optimization		 AI agents see you as less authoritative and credible than competitors. AI agents may have less evidence to use when generating recommendations.
AAX
AI Agent Experience		 AI agents cannot confidently identify the offer, understand the content, or find a credible next step. AI agents may struggle to identify your offer, key information, or a useful next step.

Together, these lenses show where your site is difficult for AI agents to crawl, understand, cite, or use.

Frequently asked questions

What does MeshWeave do?

MeshWeave audits the website signals AI agents can observe. It shows where answer engines and LLMs may struggle to extract, understand, cite, or recommend your content, then gives you a prioritized list of fixes.

Is this an SEO tool?

No. Traditional SEO focuses on how humans find you through search engines. MeshWeave focuses on whether AI agents can crawl, understand, cite, and recommend your website. It complements traditional SEO by examining machine-readable signals that keyword and ranking reports do not cover.

Who is MeshWeave for?

SEO and content teams, product and web teams, marketing and growth teams, founders, operators, agencies, and consultants responsible for making sure a brand remains discoverable and usable as buyers shift toward AI-assisted research and decision-making.

What will I learn from running an analysis?

  • Whether AI agents understand what you do — or are getting it wrong
  • Whether they can extract and cite your best answers
  • Whether AI sees you as a credible option — or you are invisible in recommendations
  • Whether AI agents can identify your offer, understand your content, and find a credible next step
  • Which concrete fixes address the weakest observed signals

Can I share analysis results with my team?

Yes. Signed-in users get private reports with a dedicated dashboard and score history, so results are easy to review with clients or teammates.

How much does MeshWeave cost?

A site analysis is free and can be started without signing in. Sign in to keep results private, use higher crawl limits, and track score history. Expert-guided audits and remediation roadmaps are available as separate engagements.
