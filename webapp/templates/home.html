Turn a crawl into a focused fix list

MeshWeave examines rendered pages, structured data, metadata, content structure, crawler access, entity consistency, and contact signals. The report connects those observations to the three ways AI systems can fail: extracting an answer, recognizing authority, or identifying a useful next step.

01

We show you the evidence

We render your pages and inspect the content, metadata, structured data, links, crawler directives, and contact information that AI agents can access.

02

We identify the failure

We group findings under the three checks — Reachable, Answerable, and Actionable — then rank them by signal severity and expected score impact. The report makes no claims about outside outcomes.

03

We hand you the fix

You get concrete recommendations tied to the observed signal, such as missing schema, weak structure, blocked crawlers, inconsistent entity descriptions, or unclear contact paths.

What changes when you fix it

Use the report to fix what blocks AI agents on your own site. Scores are diagnostic measures of the website itself, not guarantees of outside outcomes.

Make answers extractable

Make important answers easier for AI agents to extract by improving structure, metadata, and machine-readable context.

Give agents the whole picture

Strengthen the entity, trust, crawl-access, and content signals so AI agents can reconcile your business identity and evidence across the site.

Prove the change

Compare your current report with later runs to see which findings were resolved and where the site still isn't AI-friendly.

Be agent-ready

Clarify your offer, cross-page relationships, metadata, and contact paths so AI agents can identify a credible next step. The actionability check does not test transactions or operate an interactive browser agent.

Three ways AI can fail to use your website

Reachable measures machine context, Answerable measures answer extractability, and Actionable measures whether a structured AI evaluation can understand your offer and identify a credible next step. Together, they show what the crawl can verify and what still needs human observation.

Risk Check What Failure Looks Like What You Fix
Reachable
Machine context		 Inaccessible content or inconsistent site context — agents can't reach the material or reconcile what the business is. Crawl access, structured data, and consistent identity and evidence across the site.
Answerable
Answer extractability		 Unsupported answers — the copy is empty or ambiguous and doesn't back a direct answer. Clear, well-structured content that supports direct answers agents can extract.
Actionable
Agent actionability		 Missing action path — agents can't confidently identify the offer, understand the content, or find a credible next step. A clear offer and contact paths so agents can identify what to do next.

Together, these checks show where your site is difficult for AI agents to reach, answer from, or act on.

Frequently asked questions

What does MeshWeave do?

MeshWeave audits the website signals AI agents can observe. It shows where AI agents may struggle to reach or extract your content, or find a supported answer from it, then gives you a prioritized list of fixes.

Is this an SEO tool?

No. Traditional SEO focuses on how humans find you through search engines. MeshWeave focuses on whether AI agents can reach your website, answer from its content, and act on it. It complements traditional SEO by examining machine-readable signals that keyword and ranking reports do not cover.

Who is MeshWeave for?

SEO and content teams, product and web teams, marketing and growth teams, founders, operators, agencies, and consultants responsible for making sure a brand remains discoverable and usable as buyers shift toward AI-assisted research and decision-making.

What will I learn from running an analysis?

  • Whether agents reach your content — or miss it
  • Whether your copy supports real answers — or leaves them unsupported
  • Whether the site gives agents a credible next step — or forces a dead end
  • Which concrete fixes address the weakest observed signals

Can I share analysis results with my team?

Yes. Signed-in users get private reports with a dedicated dashboard and score history, so results are easy to review with clients or teammates.

How much does MeshWeave cost?

A site analysis is free and can be started without signing in. Sign in to keep results private, use higher crawl limits, and track score history. Expert-guided audits and remediation roadmaps are available as separate engagements.
