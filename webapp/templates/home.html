Frequently asked questions

What are AEO, GEO, and AAX scores?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) measures how well your site captures Google AI Overviews and featured snippets.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) measures how well AI engines like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can find, understand, and recommend your content.
AAX (AI Agent Experience) measures how well AI agents can navigate, understand, and interact with your site — including crawler access, brand comprehension, and contactability.

Why does AI search matter?

Traditional search engines show a list of links. AI engines give direct answers — and they choose which sources to cite. AEO optimizes for Google's AI Overviews and featured snippets. GEO optimizes for ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. AAX optimizes for AI agents that act on behalf of users. If you're not optimized for all three, you're missing the fastest-growing channel for discovery.

What data do you need?

Just a URL. The engine auto-scores schema coverage, content structure, entity signals, LLM crawl accessibility, and content depth. Optionally add Search Console data or voice results for a complete score.

Are results public or private?

Signed-in users: private by default. Anonymous runs: public. Public analyses can be claimed after signing in.

Can results be shared?

Yes. Each analysis has a unique URL. Shared pages show score gauges and factor breakdowns — no account needed.
