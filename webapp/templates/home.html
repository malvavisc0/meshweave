What does our engine do?

It reveals how AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI see your website — what they understand, what they can cite, and what's invisible to them. Then it tells you exactly what to fix to increase your visibility and citation rate.

Traditional SEO is built for a world where customers click blue links. That world is disappearing. MeshWeave is proactive defense against the AI shift — it tells you whether AI systems recommend you at all, what they can't read, and what to fix before your competitors lock in the citations.
